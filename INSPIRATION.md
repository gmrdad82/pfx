The voice effects skip two sources on licence: Faust's `ve.vocoder` sits in the LGPL section of `vaeffects.lib`, and aubio's onsets are GPL; neither was used.

[pissang/oidn-web](https://github.com/pissang/oidn-web) (MIT), a WebGPU runtime for the same weights, was read for its channel-blocked layout; no code was taken from it.

The sim input journal (`crates/core/src/sim/journal.rs`) takes its varint and zigzag from [Protocol Buffers' wire encoding](https://protobuf.dev/programming-guides/encoding/), its change mask with per-field deltas from Quake 3's delta-compressed snapshots, and its seed-plus-inputs log from Doom's `.lmp` demos; formulas only, no code taken. The text generator (`crates/core/src/words.rs`) follows the slot-template grammar of [Tracery](https://github.com/galaxykate/tracery) (Apache-2.0); the idea only, no code taken. The PCM stream's ring (`crates/sound/src/pcm.rs`) follows [rtrb](https://github.com/mgeier/rtrb) (MIT OR Apache-2.0): a fixed buffer, a head and a tail in Acquire and Release order, positions counted to twice the length so a full ring differs from an empty one; the layout only, no code taken.
